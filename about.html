About me

The average person spends 30,000 days on Earth. Across my lifetime, I want to create enduring works that I can proudly share with the world while maximizing the ephemeral beauty of meeting exceptional people.

I was born in Shenyang, grew up in Vancouver since age 3, and graduated from Cornell in 2026. In my free time, I follow FC Barcelona and the Carolina Hurricanes, read, and swim long distances.
