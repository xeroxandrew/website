I want to spend my life building cool things with my friends — mostly tools for investors, and occasionally writing about what I learn along the way.

Right now I'm building

  • Pulse: an AI Excel agent for hedge funds and public‑market research.
  • Scrapers + signal pipelines (LMS adoption, RFPs, pricing, and “proof of adoption” evidence).
  • Open‑source + small experiments in automation and research workflows.

And learning

  • How to design products that feel simple (even when the plumbing isn't).
  • How to write clearly about investing, data, and building.
  • How to ship faster and with fewer “almost done” projects.
